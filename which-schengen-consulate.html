Which Schengen consulate should I apply to?

Apply to the country that is your main destination, not automatically the country where your plane lands. First entry is the fallback when you cannot determine a main destination by the purpose or length of your stay.

Apply the Article 5 rules in order

  1. One destination: Apply to that Schengen country.
  2. Several destinations: Identify the main destination by the purpose or length of the stay (measured in days). If you’re taking the same kind of holiday in each country, usually that means the longest stay. A specific reason for the trip, such as the conference you’re travelling to attend, can establish the main destination even if you spend longer sightseeing elsewhere.
  3. No main destination: Apply to the country whose external Schengen border you will cross first. Equal stays only trigger this fallback if your purpose doesn’t establish a main destination.

Three examples

  • Same purpose, different lengths: You land in the Netherlands, spend three full days there and seven full days in France, all for tourism. Apply to France.
  • Clear purpose: Your trip is to attend a conference in Germany for two days, followed by five days sightseeing in Italy. Germany can be the main destination because the conference is the reason for the trip. Include the registration or invitation and the whole itinerary.
  • True tie: You have equal-length holiday stays in Spain and Portugal with no other main purpose and you clear Schengen passport control in Spain first. Apply to Spain.

Find the office that handles your address

Article 5 identifies the country; Article 6 normally puts the application in the district where you legally reside. For example, if France is your main destination and you legally reside in the UAE, start with the France checklist for UAE residents. A country may be represented by another Schengen country or use an authorized application centre. Check the destination’s official website for the office that covers your address. If you’re legally present somewhere but don’t live there, ask the mission first: Article 6 requires justification for lodging there.

Double-check any connecting flight if first entry matters. Use the airport where you actually clear Schengen passport control, not an airport where you remain in international transit. Keep the itinerary, bookings and purpose evidence consistent; don’t change the application country just to find a faster appointment.

If your main destination is Italy and you live in Saudi Arabia, see the Italy checklist for Saudi residents. If it’s Spain and you live in the Philippines, see the Spain checklist for Philippine residents.

Sources: EU Visa Code, Article 5, Article 6 and the European Commission’s application guide. This guide concerns short-stay visas; confirm the filing location and checklist with the official mission before you apply.
