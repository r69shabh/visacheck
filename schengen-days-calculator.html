How to use a Schengen 90/180-day calculator

Use the European Commission’s free short-stay calculator to check past dates or plan your next entry. If you’re preparing a visa application, the VisaCheck file checker also checks your planned trip against the 90/180-day limit alongside your bookings and documents.

Gather the dates that matter

  1. Write down your actual entry and exit dates at the Schengen Area boundary. Use local dates from travel records, not the day you left home or the night you checked out of a hotel.
  2. Include short stays that overlap the 179 days before your planned entry. A visit that started earlier can still contribute days. Enter one date range for each continuous stay, even if you visited several Schengen countries during it.
  3. Count arrival and departure as Schengen days. For example, 5–12 August is eight days, not seven. Do not add dates when you were wholly outside Schengen.

Choose the right mode

Planning: In the EU calculator, enter the intended date of entry and your past stays. Compare its result with your intended departure. For a trip with several exits and re-entries, check each leg and include the earlier planned legs when calculating a later entry.

Check: In the EU calculator, enter the date you want to check and the dates of completed or ongoing stays. In VisaCheck, enter the proposed trip dates under Trip & itinerary, then add each prior date range under Recent Schengen stays. The result shows the maximum number of days counted in a rolling window during the proposed trip when your history is complete.

What if the dates show a possible overstay?

For a future trip, try an earlier departure or a later entry, then run the check again. A single check at the end of a trip is not a substitute for checking each day: the window can lose older days before you leave. See the worked 90/180 examples for why the total changes.

If you may already have overstayed or may be unable to leave in time, contact the competent immigration authority where you are for case-specific guidance. A calculator does not decide your legal status or extend your permission. If your visa sticker authorizes fewer than 90 days, follow that shorter duration as well as its dates and entry limits; the EU calculator is not intended to check that shorter allowance.

For application requirements, consult the European Commission’s visa application guide and your destination’s consulate.
