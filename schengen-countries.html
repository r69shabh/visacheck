VisaCheck / Schengen countries

Schengen countries: all 29 members

The Schengen Area includes 25 EU countries and four non-EU countries. Bulgaria and Romania are full members: internal air and sea border checks were lifted on 31 March 2024, followed by internal land border checks on 1 January 2025.

EU countries in Schengen

Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Czechia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, the Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain and Sweden.

Non-EU countries in Schengen

Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway and Switzerland.

Schengen is not the same as the EU

Ireland and Cyprus are in the EU but not in the Schengen Area. The United Kingdom is in neither. Check each non-Schengen destination’s own entry and transit rules; membership of the EU alone does not tell you whether a visa is valid there.

How the list affects your itinerary

  • France to Switzerland: both are in Schengen, so your short-stay count continues even though Switzerland is not in the EU.
  • Romania to Bulgaria: both are in Schengen. Count the date once, not once per country.
  • Spain to Ireland: you leave Schengen. Count the date you leave Spain as a Schengen day; subsequent days spent entirely in Ireland don’t count toward Schengen’s limit.

Check your exact destination if you’re visiting an overseas territory: the European country’s membership does not necessarily mean the same entry rules apply there. Also carry the required travel documents within Schengen; countries can temporarily reintroduce internal border checks.
