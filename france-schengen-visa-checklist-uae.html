France Schengen visa document checklist from the UAE

Use this checklist if you legally live in the United Arab Emirates, need a short-stay tourist visa and France is your main destination. The French mission in the UAE directs applicants to France-Visas for the application, supporting documents and filing instructions.

Review your trip and documents in the free VisaCheck checker.

Prepare your France-Visas file

  1. Start at the French mission’s UAE visa page. Open its visa assistant and enter your nationality, the UAE as your place of residence and tourism as your purpose. Use the resulting document list and follow the official online application and filing steps.
  2. Set aside your passport, photo and signed application. Keep the France-Visas application receipt with the file and check the generated list for how many copies to bring. If you aren’t a UAE citizen, have your UAE residence evidence ready and check its required format and validity.
  3. Show your route into and out of Schengen and where you’ll stay each night. If you’ll stay with a private host in France, use the visa assistant to check whether you need an attestation d’accueil (official host certificate) instead of a hotel booking. Don’t use a hotel booking for a stay you actually intend to spend with a host.
  4. Gather proof of how you’ll fund the trip and why you’ll return to your country of residence. If someone else is paying, check what proof the assistant requests from you and the sponsor. Check locally how recent the records need to be.
  5. Obtain your €30,000 travel medical insurance certificate. Match your passport name, all Schengen destinations and the entire trip, including the exit date.

Before booking your appointment: Reopen the UAE filing instructions from the French mission page and use the centre and process it names. A UAE visit stamp is not the same as UAE legal residence. If you don’t live there, confirm where you can lodge before paying any fees.

Sources: French mission in the UAE (in French), France-Visas (English) and EU Visa Code, Article 14. This is a preparation checklist; follow the official, personalized list for your appointment, including any additional evidence for minors or sponsorship.
