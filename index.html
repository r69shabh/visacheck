THE FREE FILE CHECK

Put your trip together.

For applicants worldwide who need a short-stay visa for tourism, business or family visits. Not legal or visa advice; not affiliated with any embassy, visa centre or the EU.

01

Applicant & passport

Use the details on the passport you plan to travel with.

02

Trip & itinerary

Use the local dates of your first Schengen entry and final exit. List each overnight stop.

Itinerary stops

One row per stop. The next stop begins on the previous stop’s end date. Add a zero-night final stop if you change cities on exit day.

Recent Schengen stays

Count short stays in the previous 179 days, not stays authorized under a residence permit or long-stay visa.

03

Bookings & insurance

Compare your planned stays and flights to the evidence you’ll submit.

Accommodation

Include each hotel or private host stay covering nights inside Schengen.

Travel medical insurance

Use the coverage limit, not the premium. For another currency, enter how many units of that currency equal €1. Use the ECB reference table where available, or ask your insurer for the equivalent in euros.

04

Supporting documents

Mark the documents you’ve already prepared. Always check your mission’s latest list.
